# The name and the art of Wondrous Compass

The [MIT License](LICENSE) covers the code of this addon. It gives no right to the name **Wondrous Compass**, to its
compass logo, to its launcher and store icons or to the pictures of its store listing. Those stay Yesanith's.

You may fork the code, change it and publish what you made, as the license says. A copy that is published, in a store, as
an APK, as a site or in any other way, has to:

- have a name of its own, that cannot be taken for Wondrous Compass;
- use a logo and icons of its own;
- not say or suggest that it is Wondrous Compass, a version of it, or made or approved by its author;
- not use this project's backend (its Supabase project, its Discord bot or its functions) as if it were its own.

A copy that does not is reported to the store or the host it is on.

## The official builds

Wondrous Compass comes only from these places:

- the Android app: the [releases of wc-frontend](https://github.com/Yesanith/wc-frontend/releases/latest), and the
  Google Play listing linked from the site;
- the browser app: [yesanith.github.io/wc-frontend/app](https://yesanith.github.io/wc-frontend/app/);
- the in-game addons: Wondrous Compass for Nexus, from the [releases of this repository](https://github.com/Yesanith/wc-nexus/releases/latest)
  (the signature Nexus knows it by is `0x57434E58`), and the Blish HUD module, from its own repository.

Anything else that asks for your Guild Wars 2 API key under this name is not this app. The addon never asks for one: it is
linked with a code from the app. If you are unsure, make a key in [your ArenaNet account](https://account.arena.net/applications)
with only the permissions the app asks for, and delete it there when you stop using it.
